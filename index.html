AI E-Learning Platform

Suggests what to study next from a student's answer history. Research project by Lakshya Pandey, Vinayak Kumar and Aaryan Madhok.

How to use: answer the questions below. After every answer a classifier predicts, for each topic, whether your next answer will be correct, and labels the topic Mastered or Not yet. A* search then plans the shortest study path. Mastery is the model's estimate, not a test score, and the "what if" numbers are not causal. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. See How it works for results and limitations.

3. Shortest study path (A* search)

A* finds the fewest practice questions after which the classifier labels every weak topic you have practised as Mastered. Each step = answer one question; the plan assumes the answers are correct, so it is the minimum.
